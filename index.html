Craft & Heritage

Rooted in passion, brewed for Mumbai

Ember & Bean began as a humble backyard roaster in 2012 with a vintage cast-iron drum and an obsession with single-origin Indian coffees. Today, we bridge the gap between conscientious mountain farmers and discerning city coffee lovers. Every batch is roasted in small quantities to highlight the distinct terroir of India’s highland plantations, bringing warmth and uncompromising quality to every cup we pour.
